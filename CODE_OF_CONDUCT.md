# Code of conduct

AutoMono follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, keep discussion about the work, and respect that people here
write in Spanish and English with different levels of fluency. Harassment, insults and personal
attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/aryan-curiel). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code,
following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
